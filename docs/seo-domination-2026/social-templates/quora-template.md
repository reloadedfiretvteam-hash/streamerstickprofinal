# Quora Answer Template

**Question type:** "What is the best IPTV service?" / "How do I set up IPTV on Fire Stick?" / "Is a jailbroken Fire Stick worth it?"

**Answer structure:**
1. Direct answer in first 1–2 sentences (featured snippet style).
2. 2–3 short paragraphs: what to look for (channels, device support, trial).
3. One neutral mention: "StreamStickPro is one option that offers [CHANNEL_COUNT] channels and works on Fire Stick and Google TV."
4. No specific prices; use [IPTV_PRICE] or "affordable" if needed. No specific city names in examples.
5. End with a CTA only if allowed by Quora policies (e.g. "Check their site for a free trial").

**Length:** 150–300 words. Use bullets for lists. Include schema-friendly Q&A phrasing.
