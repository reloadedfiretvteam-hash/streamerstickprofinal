# Deliverable 18: Money Page Conversion Funnels

## Funnel 1: Home → Trial / Shop

- **Landing:** / (MainStore)
- **Clicks:** Hero CTA, "Free trial", "Shop"
- **Next:** / (trial form/section) or /shop
- **End:** Checkout (Stripe), success / credentials

## Funnel 2: Pillar → Shop / Trial

- **Landing:** /iptv-services, /jailbroken-fire-sticks, /iptv-firestick, etc.
- **Clicks:** In-content links to /shop, /free-trial, "View plans"
- **Next:** /shop or /
- **End:** Checkout, success

## Funnel 3: Location page → Pillar → Shop

- **Landing:** /l/usa/iptv/houston (and all location pages)
- **Clicks:** Internal links to pillar (e.g. /iptv-services), then "Shop" / "Free trial"
- **Next:** Pillar or /shop or /
- **End:** Checkout, success

## Funnel 4: Blog / Guide → Shop

- **Landing:** /blog, /blog/:slug
- **Clicks:** CTA and inline links to /shop, /iptv-firestick, /jailbroken-fire-sticks
- **Next:** /shop or pillar
- **End:** Checkout, success

## Optimization

- Every pillar and location page: clear CTA block (e.g. "Ready to stream?" with buttons to /, /shop, /jailbroken-fire-sticks).
- Trust signals near CTA (channel count, trial, support).
- No specific prices in copy; use [IPTV_PRICE] / [STICK_PRICE] or "View pricing" link to shop.
