# Deliverable 1: Sitemap (25K URLs) + robots.txt

## Sitemap

- **Live URL:** `https://streamstickpro.com/sitemap.xml` (generated by Cloudflare Worker).
- **Index (for 50K+ URLs):** `https://streamstickpro.com/sitemap-index.xml`.
- **Limits (Google/Bing/Yandex):** 50,000 URLs per sitemap, 10MB uncompressed. Use sitemap index when exceeding.

### Contents included

- Static: `/`, `/shop`, `/blog`, `/free-trial`, pillar pages, terms/privacy/refund, checkout.
- Blog: every published post from `blog_posts`.
- Location pages: every published row from `seo_architecture` (path `/l/{country}/{page_type}/{slug}`), up to 25,000.

### Priority and lastmod

- Home: 1.0, daily.
- Shop/pillars/free-trial: 0.9, weekly.
- Blog: 0.8, weekly.
- Location pages: 0.7, weekly; `lastmod` from `seo_architecture.updated_at`.

## robots.txt

- **File:** `public/robots.txt`.
- **Explicit allows:** `/`, `/blog`, `/blog/*`, `/shop`, `/products`, `/l/`, `/l/*`, pillar paths, `/free-trial`.
- **Disallows:** `/admin`, `/api/`, `/shadow-services`, `/checkout`, `/success`, tracking/query params.
- **Sitemaps:** `Sitemap: https://streamstickpro.com/sitemap.xml` and `Sitemap: https://streamstickpro.com/sitemap-index.xml`.
- **Note:** `Crawl-delay` is ignored by Google; kept only for bots that honor it (e.g. Bing/Yandex).

## Crawl budget (25K+ pages)

- Keep important pages within 2–3 clicks of homepage.
- Use internal links from pillars to location pages and money pages.
- Avoid duplicate URLs (canonicals, no session/tracking params in sitemap).
- Fix 404s and redirect chains so crawlers don’t waste budget.
