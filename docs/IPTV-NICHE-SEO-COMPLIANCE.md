## IPTV gray area (2025–2026)

- **Technology:** IPTV (internet-delivered TV) is legal. Legality depends on **content licensing**.  
- **Enforcement:** Unlicensed redistribution can be targeted (criminal/civil). Sellers and distributors face higher risk than end users.  
- **Platforms:** Google and Amazon have tightened policies; some IPTV-related sites have been deindexed or restricted. Advertising unlicensed services increases legal and platform risk.  

**StreamStickPro positioning (safe and consistent):**

- Use **“Live TV streaming,” “IPTV subscription,” “pre-configured devices,” “fully loaded”** (device ready to use).  
- Use **“18,000+ channels,” “100,000+ movies,” “sports,” “PPV”** as product features, not as “free cable” or piracy.  
- **Jailbroken Fire Sticks:** Page explains that “jailbroken” often means sideloading / pre-configured; we sell **pre-configured** devices and **subscriptions** with setup support.  
- **Legal FAQ:** Jailbroken page states sideloading is allowed by Amazon; customers should use the device within the terms of their subscription and local laws.  
- **No claims** that content is “free” or unlicensed; we sell devices + **subscription plans** and support.  
- **Payment:** Normal methods (Stripe, etc.); no “crypto only” or “anonymous only” positioning.  

This keeps the site aligned with “subscription-based Live TV and pre-configured streaming devices” and avoids language that implies unlicensed redistribution.
